# Minuto Support

Minuto must have a monitored support channel before release.

## Support requirements

The operator should publish:

- A support email address.
- A response-time target.
- A process for order cancellation and refunds.
- A process for payment disputes and duplicate payments.
- A process for seller payout questions.
- A process for delivery problems and missing orders.
- A process for account deletion requests.

Replace the placeholders below before submitting Minuto to Google Play:

```text
Support email: support@example.com
Support URL: https://example.com/minuto/support
Privacy URL: https://example.com/minuto/privacy
```

Never request passwords, Supabase service-role keys, YouPI webhook secrets, or one-time authentication codes through support.
